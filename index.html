    5 rules that stop most UPI frauds

    PIN is only to SEND moneyYou never enter your UPI PIN to receive money.
    Never share OTPNot with the bank, not with "customer care", not with anyone.
    "Approve request" = money goes outDo not approve collect requests from strangers.
    Do not install apps from linksAnyDesk, TeamViewer or APK files give scammers control.
    Lost money? Act fastCall 1930 (National Cyber Crime Helpline) and report at cybercrime.gov.in. Also tell your bank.
